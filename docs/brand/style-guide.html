A burgundy atelier for a pink logo.

Three colour directions and three font pairings for the Lulu Femme website, built around the existing LF logo, which stays exactly as it is. Every colour pair is contrast-checked, and every value ships as CSS, SCSS and JSON tokens.

01 · Constraints

The logo sets the rules

The logo is not changing, so the palette is built to suit it.

  • Single colour: logo pink #E3165B (RGB 227, 22, 91), a cool magenta-red. It is never recoloured.
  • Monogram: heavy geometric strokes with large rounded corners (about 15% of stroke length).
  • Wordmark: wide, extended geometric sans in caps with generous letter-spacing; about 14:1 width to height.
  • Because the logo is pink and only pink, every background it sits on must give it at least 3:1 contrast (WCAG 1.4.11, graphical objects).
Allowed groundsIvory, warm white, blush and the dark-mode Night colour. All give the pink logo at least 3:1.
NeverBurgundy (2.28:1) or muted rose (1.59:1) behind the logo, and never recolour, outline or add effects to it. On photos, place it on an ivory plate.
Clear space & sizeKeep clear space equal to half the monogram height on every side. Minimum: monogram 24px tall, wordmark 120px wide.

Each direction assigns every role: background, neutrals, primary, secondary, accent and states. Hex and RGB values are exact.

04 · Usage

How much of each colour

Roughly 60% ground, 30% supporting neutrals and tints, 10% colour. Keep the accent under 2% so the logo stays the brightest thing on screen.

Atelier Burgundy

60% Ivory22% Blush12% Deep burgundy

Last two slivers: Muted rose (4%) and Logo pink (2%).

Rose Studio

60% Soft ivory22% Petal12% Logo pink

Last two slivers: Plum (4%) and Logo pink (2%).

Ivory Noir

60% Ivory22% Stone12% Noir

Last two slivers: Deep burgundy (4%) and Logo pink (2%).

Do

  • Page background: ivory. Product grids and forms: warm-white cards.
  • Alternate long pages with blush bands to separate sections.
  • Primary for one main action per view; secondary outline for the rest.
  • Rose ink for eyebrows and tags on ivory or white.
  • Pink ink for 'New', 'Sold' and active states, sparingly.
  • States: hover darkens one step, pressed two steps, disabled uses the disabled pair.

Don't

  • Don't set text in muted rose (2.6:1) or the logo pink below 24px.
  • Don't put the logo on burgundy or rose.
  • Don't use colour alone for errors; always add an icon and a message.
  • Don't put rose ink text on blush (4.0:1); use burgundy there.
  • Don't mix directions on one page.

  • The moodboard pairing. Cormorant's high-contrast Garamond forms say 'curated' and 'atelier' for headings and the brand voice.
  • Manrope is a geometric grotesk with open apertures that stays clear at 12–14px for prices, forms and navigation.
  • Set Manrope in caps with 0.16–0.18em tracking for eyebrows and nav: it echoes the logo's wide, spaced wordmark without copying it.

Watch out: Cormorant has a small x-height and fine hairlines. Never use it below 24px or for body text; use weight 600 under 40px.

09 · Rollout

Implementation checklist

  1. DecidePick the direction (Atelier Burgundy recommended) and font pairing; sign off this guide.
  2. FontsLoad Cormorant Garamond 500/600/500i and Manrope 400–700 with next/font; remove Anton. Set font-display: swap and real fallback stacks.
  3. TokensReplace the colour variables in app/globals.css with tokens.css values (mapping table below). Keep names stable so components don't change.
  4. LogoDo not edit logo files. Audit every place the logo appears: bg, surface or tint only. Never on burgundy, rose or photos without an ivory plate.
  5. Buttons & linksPrimary = burgundy fill / ivory text; secondary = burgundy outline; links burgundy with 1px underline; hover links in pink ink.
  6. FocusOne focus style everywhere: 3px logo-pink outline, 2px offset. Check it on every interactive element with the keyboard.
  7. Forms & statesField borders use border-strong (≥3:1). Errors: garnet text + border + icon + message. Success: sage text + icon.
  8. Muted roseSearch for rose used as text or icons and swap to rose ink. Keep rose only for fills and decoration.
  9. Dark modeApply the Atelier dark tokens; check the logo on Night (4.0:1) and every pairing in the contrast table.
  10. TestRun the axe test suite (npm run test:a11y) and review home, bundle, quote and about pages at 375, 768, 1024 and 1440px.
  11. LaunchUpdate Open Graph images and favicon background to ivory; clear the CDN cache; spot-check on a real phone.
